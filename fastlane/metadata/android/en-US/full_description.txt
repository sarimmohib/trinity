Trinity replaces three stock apps on your phone - Phone, Messages and Contacts - with a single app that does all three, in one look.

It is open source, with no ads, no trackers and no telemetry, and it needs no account. Everything it shows comes from your own phone: the call log, your SMS and your contacts.

CALLS
Your call log, grouped by person, with direction, duration and time. A dial pad, and in-call controls: mute, speaker, keypad and hang up. Incoming calls show who is calling even when the number arrives in a different format than the one saved in your contacts. When a call ends, a short summary tells you whether it was answered, missed, declined, busy or failed, how long it lasted and who hung up.

MESSAGES
Conversations and threads grouped by person, quick reply from the notification, sending, forwarding, copying and deleting, muting a conversation, and starting a new message from a number.

CONTACTS
Your address book with per-person call and message history. Add, edit and delete contacts, set a contact photo straight into the phone's contacts - Trinity keeps no copy. WhatsApp availability when WhatsApp shares its contacts, with chat and call shortcuts, and an indicator that says whether your contacts are actually syncing.

BLOCKING
Name and number rules hide a whole conversation and can silence its calls. Keyword rules flag or hide individual messages. Combined rules join conditions with AND / OR, for example a name and a word in the text. Blocked texts are held aside instead of deleted, so you can see what was caught and let a sender through later. Rules can apply to calls only, messages only, or both.

Trinity needs Android 8.0 or newer and replaces the Phone, Messages and Contacts roles, so Android asks you to confirm which app handles calls, SMS and contacts.
